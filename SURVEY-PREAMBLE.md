# Preamble for the survey form

Text to paste into the Google Form, in the section that carries the link. Two
versions: the full one, and a short one if the form is already long.

Both exist to prevent the same four things — a respondent stopped by ngrok's
warning page, a respondent trying to register an account, a respondent seeing
only half the system, and a respondent who finds the link dead and assumes the
system is broken rather than that the laptop is off.

---

## Brief version

The one actually used on the form. Everything the longer versions say, in the
four sentences a respondent will actually read.

> Below is the link to our system.
>
> It opens on a page that gives you the sign-in details and a short list of
> things to try, so there is no need to register.
>
> Nothing on the system is real, so please explore freely. Kindly return to this
> form afterwards.

The "click Visit Site" line is gone because the Cloudflare address does not
show one. Put it back only if the link in the form is the ngrok address, which
does.

---

## Full version

**Before you begin**

The link below opens a working copy of the system. It runs on the researchers'
computer, so please open it at a time when you are ready to try it — it is not
available at all hours.

**What to expect**

1. The first page you see is a security notice from the service that makes the
   system reachable online. Click **Visit Site** to continue. It appears only
   once.

2. You will then land on a page titled *Thank you for testing this system*. It
   contains everything you need: the sign-in details, a button for each part of
   the system, and a short list of things worth trying.

3. **You do not need to register.** Accounts are provided for you on that page,
   and a button fills the sign-in form in for you.

**There are two sides to try**

The system has a customer side — the online shop, where you browse products,
ask the assistant questions and place an order — and a business side, the back
office, where staff manage stock, orders, payments and deliveries. The landing
page gives you a login for each, and they open in separate tabs so you can move
between them freely.

**Please note**

Nothing on the system is real. No payment is processed and no order is ever
delivered. You are welcome to place orders, change records and explore as
freely as you like — nothing you do can cause any harm.

Around 10 to 15 minutes is enough to see both sides comfortably.

When you have finished, please return to this form to answer the questions.

If the link does not open at all, the system is temporarily offline. Kindly try
again later, or contact the researchers.
